## Làm
1. Tạo model `User` (passwordHash `select:false`, `toJSON` bỏ field nhạy cảm), `Publisher`, `Author`, `Category`, `Book`.
2. Khai báo đủ index: `email`, `code`, `{role,status}`, text index user; `Book`: text `{title,authorText}` với `default_language:'none'`, `slug`, `code`, `{categories}`, `{authors}`, `{borrowCount:-1}`, `{createdAt:-1}`.
3. `Book`: validate `0 ≤ availableCopies ≤ totalCopies`; `timestamps:true` mọi schema.
